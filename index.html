Features

Everything needed to manage complaints end to end.

Easy submission

Report issues with category, location and priority. Get a unique reference instantly.

Live tracking

A full status timeline shows who changed what, and when.

Smart assignment

Admins route complaints to departments and can reassign at any time.

Reports and charts

Statistics calculated from real data, plus CSV export.

Notifications

Users are notified on every assignment and status change.

Three roles

Separate dashboards for users, administrators and department staff.

How It Works

Four simple steps.

1

Submit

A user raises a complaint.

2

Assign

The admin picks a department.

3

Work

Staff update progress with remarks.

4

Resolve

Resolution notes close the complaint.
